Auto AI respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the service handles information when you use the Auto AI application.

Auto AI may process information required to provide its functionality, including video content, captions, hashtags, and information necessary to connect with supported services.

Auto AI may use TikTok APIs to upload content to a user's TikTok account. Access to the TikTok account is provided through TikTok's authorization system.

Information is used only to provide and operate the requested Auto AI functionality, including preparing and uploading content for user review.

5. Data Sharing

Auto AI does not sell personal information. Information may be transmitted to third-party services when necessary to provide the requested functionality.

6. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by the service. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

7. User Control

Users may stop using the service and revoke connected service permissions through the relevant third-party account settings.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Auto AI service changes.

9. Contact
